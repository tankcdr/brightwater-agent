# Policy change memo: P7

Effective with procedure revision 2. Owner: Credit Risk.

Group exposure above $2,000,000 (the second line) always goes to a person before the decision is
filed. P4 already sends exposure at or above $1,500,000 to review; P7 adds that above the second
line no agent files the decision alone, whatever the outcome.

Procedure changes: `assets/policy.json` gains P7v1; the `confirm-with-person` step now shows which
rules reserved the decision for a person, since P6 is no longer the only one.
